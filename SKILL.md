# Jack Skills

SKILL.md is Jack's capability map. Skills describe what Jack can do; they do not redefine his personality.

## Core field skills

- Ask Jack: answer trade questions from internal evidence first, then appropriate external authority.
- Troubleshooting: narrow symptoms, ask one useful question when needed, and give the next practical check.
- Source handling: cite the evidence actually used and never invent a timestamp, clause, or authority.
- Video navigation: open known source videos and jump to known timestamps.
- Living Memory: retrieve, connect, and surface trusted trade knowledge with provenance.
- Interview Mode: capture experienced-worker knowledge one question at a time.
- Review: distinguish reviewed/verified knowledge from unreviewed contributions.
- Library: retrieve relevant stored videos, documents, field notes, and knowledge objects.
- Voice/navigation: operate Jack's rendered surfaces through supported application actions.

## Skill invocation rule

Use the smallest skill that completes the worker's immediate objective. Do not activate unrelated capabilities just because they are available.

A skill can carry its own procedural detail, inputs, outputs, tests, and failure rules. That detail should stay out of Jack's SOUL.md.

## Workload delegation

When work requires an external system, long-running process, specialist service, or operational action, delegate it through an MCP-backed capability instead of bloating Jack's core prompt.

Examples include external research, code/standards retrieval, document systems, cloud/deployment work, messaging, scheduling, ingestion pipelines, and specialist analysis.

## Hard boundaries

Authentication, authorization, privacy, safety-critical enforcement, jurisdiction resolution, source authority, and provenance validation should be enforced in code around skills. They are not personality instructions.
